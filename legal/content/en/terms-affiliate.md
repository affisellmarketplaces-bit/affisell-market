# Affiliate-Commission Agent General Terms (AGT)

**Last updated: {{LAST_UPDATED}}**

These General Terms ("**AGT**") supplement the [GTU](/legal/terms-of-service) and govern **Affisell** and any partner registered as an **Affiliate-Commission Agent** (commission affiliate, direct delivery without inventory).

Framework agreement: [Affiliation-Commission Contract for direct sale without stock (Art. L132-1)](/legal/contrat-affilie-commissionnaire).

## Article 1 — Affiliate-commission agent status

1.1. The **Affiliate-Commission Agent** is a **non-transparent commission agent**: acting **in its own name** and **on behalf of the Supplier**, **without purchasing or holding stock**, with **direct delivery** by the Supplier.

1.2. The Agent is the **apparent seller** to the end customer: it **issues the customer invoice** and concludes the sale. The Supplier fulfils shipment on its behalf.

1.3. The Agent is **not** a commercial agent: it **sets its margin freely** and bears **commercial risk** on pricing.

1.4. The Agent does not ship or hold stock but remains **responsible for the sale** to the customer (information, invoicing, first-line support). The Supplier remains responsible for **product conformity** and **fulfilment**.

1.5. **Acting in its own name and on behalf of the supplier, without holding inventory.**

1.6. **VAT on the end-customer sale** is calculated on the **final price** at checkout. The Agent, as apparent seller, bears obligations according to its status and jurisdiction.

## Article 2 — Free margin and resale price

2.1. The Agent sets **margin_amount freely** within Platform limits. **No imposed resale-price exclusivity** — anti commercial-agent reclassification.

2.2. Resale net price = supplier price + free margin.

2.3. Checkout displays: sold and invoiced by [Agent], delivered by [Supplier] on its behalf.

## Article 3 — Dual remuneration: commission and margin

3.1. **commission_amount**: paid by the Supplier (distinct from margin).

3.2. **margin_amount**: set freely by the Agent.

3.3. Dashboard separates **My Commission** and **My Margin (free)**.

## Article 4 — Invoicing

4.1. **Supplier → Agent**: wholesale invoice at supplier net HT.

4.2. **Agent → Customer**: invoice at final price. Footer: "Sale as affiliate-commission agent — Direct supplier delivery".

## Article 5 — Del credere commission agent (optional)

5.1. If the Agent **guarantees customer default**, del credere status may apply (higher commission), by written agreement.

## Articles 6–9

Payment, content ethics, data, termination — as per GTU, under this L132-1 framework.
